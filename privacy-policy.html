Namaz One — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Namaz One is committed to protecting your personal data. The app does not require an account, and we do not store your personal information on our servers. The data controller is Emre Yurt, contactable at namazoneapp@gmail.com.

Location data

Your location is used only to calculate accurate prayer times for where you are, to determine the Qibla direction, and to find mosques near you. We operate no servers of our own and keep no copy of your location.

To provide these features your coordinates are sent, over an encrypted connection, to the Aladhan API (aladhan.com) for prayer times and to the Overpass API (overpass-api.de, part of OpenStreetMap) for nearby mosques. Those requests carry no name, account or identifier, because the app has neither accounts nor logins. Where you have granted the precise location permission, the coordinates sent are precise rather than approximate.

You may decline the location permission and choose your city manually instead. Separately, Google AdMob receives your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location; this happens whether or not you grant the location permission.

Notifications

Notification permission is used only to show adhan alarms, prayer reminders and the verse of the day. It is not used for marketing.

Advertising and consent

The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and for analytics and fraud prevention, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares advertising and device identifiers, your IP address, records of how you interact with adverts and the app, and diagnostic information such as app launch time and crash rate. This data is encrypted in transit. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent choices before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change those choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data storage

Prayer tracking, the tasbih counter, missed-prayer records, favourite verses, downloaded Quran text and recitation audio, and your preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data with it. We have no ability to access, recover or export it.

Third-party services

  • Aladhan API — prayer time calculation
  • AlQuran Cloud API — Quran text and translations
  • islamic.network CDN — Quran recitation audio
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — nearby mosque data
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Services — location

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Accuracy of times and direction

Prayer times are calculated from your location using established astronomical methods and may differ by a few minutes from the times announced by your local mosque or religious authority. Where they differ, follow your local authority. The Qibla compass relies on your device's magnetic sensor and can be disturbed by nearby metal or electronics; calibrate it before relying on it.

Disclaimer

This app is a tool to support your worship, not a substitute for religious guidance. Quran text, translations and supplications come from third-party sources and are provided for convenience; for religious rulings, consult a qualified scholar. The app is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, and we accept no liability for reliance on the times, direction or content it displays, to the extent permitted by law.

Your rights

Because the app stores your data only on your own device and we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes everything. For questions about advertising data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy linked above.
